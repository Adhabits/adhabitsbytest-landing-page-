How quickly can we see results?

Most schools start seeing increased inquiries within 2-3 weeks of campaign launch. Significant enrollment growth typically happens within 2-3 months as we optimize based on data.

Do you work with all types of schools?

Yes! We work with CBSE, ICSE, IB, State Board, and International schools. We also help pre-schools, coaching centers, and higher education institutions.

What makes you different from other agencies?

We exclusively serve educational institutions. This specialization means we understand parent psychology, admission cycles, and education-specific compliance requirements better than generalist agencies.

Is there any long-term contract?

No! We believe in earning your business every month. Our Growth and Enterprise plans are month-to-month. You can pause or cancel anytime with 15 days notice.

How do you measure success?

We track everything - from impressions and clicks to actual enrollment conversions. You'll get a real-time dashboard showing cost per lead, cost per admission, and overall ROI.
